02 / METHOD

From raw event
to review signal.

A reproducible supervised-learning pipeline, with identity fields kept out of the model.

01

Generate & inspect

Seeded generator creates 6,000 Malawi-style transactions. Labels come from a documented synthetic risk process; the dataset is not provider data.

02

Prepare features

One-hot encode transaction type, channel, and KYC tier. Keep amount, hour, device/recipient changes, recent velocity, and account age as numeric inputs.

03

Train the classifier

A class-weighted Random Forest is fit on a stratified training split. Account and transaction IDs are excluded to prevent identity memorization.

04

Evaluate & score

Select the probability threshold by validation F1 within the training data, refit on all training rows, then report accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, average precision, and confusion matrix on untouched test rows.
